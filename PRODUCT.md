# MiCoin

Mobile wallet/app built with Expo (SDK 54), Expo Router, NativeWind, and Better Auth.

## Platform

adaptive

## Audience

People who need a simple, trustworthy place to see and move their coins.

## Auth

- Public: `/`, `/login`, `/register`
- Private: everything else
- Better Auth client only; backend at `EXPO_PUBLIC_API_URL`
